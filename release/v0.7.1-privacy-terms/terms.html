TERMS OF USE

Use FragileFit as a planning and recordkeeping tool.

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

By using FragileFit, you agree to use the application responsibly and to review these limitations. FragileFit is an early web application that provides packaging calculations, cost estimates, business recordkeeping and summaries based on information entered by the user.

1. What the application provides

FragileFit is an informational tool. It is not a courier tariff database, marketplace service, accounting system, tax adviser, legal adviser, laboratory packaging test or guarantee that a parcel will arrive safely. Calculations depend on the dimensions, weights, rates, fees, settings and other information you enter.

2. Your information and local workspace

In the current release, workspace records are stored in the browser profile on the device you use. There are no individual FragileFit login accounts, shared team workspaces or automatic cloud synchronization. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to view and use its local workspace.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and browser profile, checking records for accuracy and keeping independent backups of important information.

3. Optional Google Drive backup

You may connect your own Google account and manually upload encrypted backup files to that account's Google Drive. You control the account and must ensure it has sufficient storage. Google authorization and Drive services are also subject to Google's terms and policies.

Backup files are encrypted in your browser before upload. You are responsible for keeping the backup password safe. FragileFit cannot recover a forgotten password. A backup restore replaces the current workspace on the device where you restore it; it does not merge the backup with existing records.

4. Accuracy and business decisions

You are responsible for verifying product measurements, packed weights, outer-carton dimensions, volumetric divisors, rounding rules, courier charges, marketplace fees, refunds, taxes and other financial inputs. Courier rules and commercial charges vary by provider, service, location and contract. Confirm them with the relevant provider.

Packaging comparisons use recorded delivery outcomes or scenario assumptions. A small, incomplete or biased sample may not predict future damage. Test packaging physically and review actual courier invoices before relying on a modelled result.

5. Acceptable use

Do not misuse the application, attempt to access another person's device or Google Drive files, interfere with the site's operation, or use the application in a way that violates applicable law or another provider's terms.

6. Availability and changes

The application is provided as an early release. Features may change, be unavailable or be discontinued. Offline availability depends on browser support and successful caching. Keep independent backups rather than relying on the website as the only copy of your records.

7. Limitation of responsibility

To the extent permitted by applicable law, FragileFit is provided without a guarantee that calculations, estimates, saved records or recommendations will be error-free or suitable for a particular business decision. You remain responsible for reviewing outputs and maintaining your own records and backups. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or protections that cannot legally be excluded.

8. Updates and contact

These terms may be revised as the application changes. The revision date appears at the top of this page. For general product support, use the FragileFit GitHub issue tracker. Do not post passwords, access tokens, backup files or private business information in public issues.
